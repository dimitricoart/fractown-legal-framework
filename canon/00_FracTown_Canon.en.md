# FracTown Canon

## Status of This Document

This document is the primary source of truth for the legal, technological, and operational architecture of the FracTown ecosystem.

All provisions of the Constitution of FracTown, marketplace rules, agreements, technical standards, API documentation, White Papers, investor materials, and any other derivative documents must comply with this Canon.

If any derivative document conflicts with this Canon, the derivative document must be amended.

---

# Canonical Principles

## 1. FracTown and FracTown Platform

**FracTown** is the global ecosystem.

**FracTown Platform** is the foundational legal and technological institution of this ecosystem.

FracTown Platform creates and maintains the unified infrastructure used by local marketplaces and other authorized participants connected through APIs or other approved technical interfaces.

---

## 2. One Object — One FCC

Each physical object registered with FracTown Platform corresponds to one and only one Fractional Crypto Certificate (FCC).

An FCC is created once and remains linked to that physical object throughout the object's entire lifecycle.

An FCC is not recreated when the object is resold, inherited, placed into custody, exhibited in a museum, or migrated between different legal or technological environments.

---

## 3. Permanent Identity of the FCC

The identity of an FCC remains unchanged throughout the entire lifecycle of the corresponding object.

Ownership history, contractual rights, transaction records, technical data, and the status of FCC fractions may change, but the identity of the FCC itself remains unchanged.

---

## 4. FCC as a Digital Representation

An FCC is a digital legal and technological certificate linked to a specific physical object.

The FCC records and represents contractual proprietary rights, transaction history, provenance information, and data required for identification of the object.

The FCC itself does not create legal title or any other proprietary right.

All legal rights arise exclusively from valid agreements entered into in accordance with applicable law.

---

## 5. Contractual Rights Prevail Over Technology

Blockchain, software, digital identifiers, databases, and any other technologies do not, by themselves, create legal rights.

Technology records, supports, and verifies contractual relationships but never replaces them.

The legal meaning of each FCC and each FCC fraction is determined exclusively by the applicable contractual framework.

---

## 6. Two Modes of FCC Circulation

Each FCC may operate in one of two circulation modes.

### Individual Ownership Mode

The physical object is sold or transferred as a whole.

The FCC accompanies the object as a single certificate.

FCC fractions exist as structural components of the certificate but remain inactive and do not circulate independently.

### Fractional Ownership Mode

The contractual framework associated with the object permits the activation and circulation of FCC fractions.

Only in this mode do FCC fractions become active units for recording and transferring contractual proprietary rights associated with the object.

A transition between these modes changes only the circulation status of the FCC and its fractions and does not change the identity of either the FCC itself or the corresponding physical object.

---

## 7. Scope of FracTown Regulation

FracTown does not regulate physical objects as such.

FracTown governs and administers the contractual proprietary rights, digital records, and FCC circulation modes associated with specific physical objects.

The physical object and the contractual rights associated with it are legally distinct but functionally interconnected elements of one system.

---

## 8. Role of FracTown Platform

FracTown Platform acts as the **Infrastructure Operator** of the ecosystem.

Upon activation of Fractional Ownership Mode, FracTown Platform additionally performs the functions of the **Collective Rights Administrator**.

In this role, the Platform provides for the protection, administration, and continuity of the contractual proprietary rights of all holders of activated FCC fractions.

---

## 9. Local Marketplaces

Local marketplaces are independent legal and commercial operators connected to FracTown Platform through APIs or other approved interfaces.

They may be owned either by companies within the FracTown ecosystem or by independent local partners.

A local marketplace provides the user-facing interface, organizes transactions, and facilitates legal and technical interaction between participants and FracTown Platform in accordance with applicable law and the terms governing its connection to the ecosystem.

No local marketplace may alter the identity of an FCC, its legal nature, or the fundamental rules governing the system.

---

## 10. Whole-Object Transactions

Most objects may be freely sold and resold as whole physical objects through local marketplaces.

Such transactions are ordinary online marketplace transactions accompanied by the corresponding FCC.

The object record and FCC record created once at the FracTown Platform level are retained and used for all subsequent resales without the need to recreate them.

---

## 11. Activation of Fractional Ownership Mode

FCC fractions remain inactive until the owner of the object initiates and legally completes the transition of the corresponding FCC into Fractional Ownership Mode.

Activation of the fractions requires an additional contractual framework defining the rights of fraction holders and providing protection for the interests of all participants in the system.

Fractional circulation begins only after the required agreements have been executed, custody of the object has been arranged, and any other required protective mechanisms have been established.

---

## 12. Protection of Minority Fraction Holders

Once an FCC has entered Fractional Ownership Mode, the physical object may be placed with a professional custodian, in a specialized secure vault, in a Freeport, in a museum, or at another approved custody location.

FracTown Platform or an authorized local marketplace may become a party to the relevant custody and control agreements.

The legal title holder may not remove, transfer, encumber, or otherwise dispose of the object in violation of the rights of FCC fraction holders or the governing contractual framework.

---

## 13. Public Exhibition

A physical object may be placed with a museum or another cultural institution for public exhibition without terminating the contractual proprietary rights represented by activated FCC fractions.

Public access to the object, private contractual rights, and the economic history of the object may coexist.

The museum or cultural institution acts as a custodian or exhibition partner and acquires no proprietary rights beyond those expressly granted under the applicable agreement.

---

## 14. Object Identification Data

FracTown Platform provides for the preservation of technical data required for future identification, comparative examination, and verification of a physical object.

Such data may include:

- ultra-high-resolution photographs;
- infrared images;
- X-ray materials;
- three-dimensional models;
- chemical analysis results;
- records concerning the physical condition of the object;
- reports issued by experts and research organizations;
- other materials relevant to identification of the object.

Identification data are linked to a specific FCC and preserved within the blockchain infrastructure of FracTown Platform or through another technologically equivalent system providing immutable and cryptographically verifiable storage.

Once identification data have been entered into the relevant repository, they may not be deleted, replaced, concealed, or altered through ordinary operations within the FracTown ecosystem.

As new inspections, expert examinations, conservation or restoration activities, or other research are carried out, additional identification materials may be added to the FCC. Each such addition creates a new and independent record and does not modify previously preserved data.

FracTown Platform must make it possible to establish:

- the content of each preserved record;
- the time at which the record was entered;
- the source of the submitted data;
- the chronological sequence in which materials were added;
- the absence of subsequent alteration of previously entered information.

If the blockchain, software, or storage method is replaced, all previously created records must be migrated while preserving their content, chronological sequence, evidence of integrity, and linkage to the corresponding FCC.

Accordingly, the identification repository creates a permanent and continuously extendable history of the physical object that may be used for comparative examination and for confirming correspondence with characteristics recorded when the FCC was created, regardless of the amount of time that has elapsed.

Preserved materials do not, by themselves, constitute an unconditional legal guarantee of authenticity. However, their immutability, provenance, and chronological continuity may be relied upon by experts, parties to transactions, courts, and other authorized persons as evidence in subsequent identification of the object and in the consideration of questions concerning its authenticity.

---

## 15. Agreements and Transaction History

Any transfer of an entire FCC or of activated FCC fractions must be supported by legally valid transaction documents.

The relevant agreements and records must be preserved in a manner that allows the complete history of the creation, modification, and transfer of contractual proprietary rights to be reconstructed at any time.

FracTown Platform maintains a permanent digital history of all FCC-related transactions regardless of the connected marketplace through which a transaction was completed.

---

## 16. Technological Neutrality

No particular blockchain, software architecture, storage method, or digital standard is a mandatory element of FracTown.

Any technology may be replaced or upgraded without terminating valid contractual proprietary rights of participants or changing the identity of the corresponding FCC.

The legal architecture of FracTown must remain resilient regardless of changes in companies, jurisdictions, or technologies.

---

## 17. No New Foundational Institutions

The foundational institutions of the FracTown ecosystem are deemed established.

Subsequent documents may define participants, their rights and obligations, procedures, and contractual mechanisms, but may not introduce new foundational institutions without formally amending both the FracTown Canon and the Constitution of FracTown.

---

## 18. FracTown Constitutional Consistency Test

Every new rule, Platform feature, agreement, business model, or technical solution must satisfy the following test.

It must:

1. Not conflict with this Canon or the Constitution of FracTown.
2. Not require creation of a new foundational institution unless objectively necessary.
3. Be capable of implementation through the existing institutions and contractual architecture.
4. Remain functional regardless of changes in company, jurisdiction, or technology.

---

# Authority of the Canon

This Canon governs the interpretation and preparation of all derivative documents within the FracTown ecosystem.

The Constitution of FracTown elaborates upon and develops the principles established by this Canon.

All operational documents implement the Constitution.

No operational document may conflict with this Canon.
