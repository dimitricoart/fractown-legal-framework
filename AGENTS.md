# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

This repository contains the official legal framework of the FracTown Platform ecosystem.

Every document in this repository must follow the FracTown Canon.

The Canon is the highest-level document of the repository.

The Constitution develops the Canon.

All remaining documents develop the Constitution.

Priority:

1. FracTown Canon
2. Constitution of FracTown
3. Legal agreements
4. Marketplace documents
5. Technical standards
6. White Paper
7. Other documents

---

## Editing Rules

Never rewrite approved text.

Never modernize language.

Never improve style.

Never summarize.

Never normalize formatting unless explicitly requested.

Approved documents are immutable.

Only explicitly requested sections may be modified.

---

## Bilingual Rule

Russian version is the source document.

English version must remain legally equivalent.

Translation may improve legal English but must never change meaning.
